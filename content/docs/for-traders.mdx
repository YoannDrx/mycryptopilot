## Application Process

4. Submit for review

## Publishing Signals

### Signal Structure

Every MyCryptoPilot signal includes:

#### Required Fields

- **Asset/Symbol**: BTC, ETH, SOL, etc.
- **Instrument Type**: SPOT or PERPETUAL
- **Bias**: LONG or SHORT
- **Entry Price**: Suggested entry level
- **Take Profit Targets**: TP1, TP2, TP3 (up to 3)
- **Stop Loss**: SL level to limit risk
- **Timeframe**: 5M, 15M, 1H, 4H, 1D, etc.
- **Risk Level**: 1-5 stars (1 = low, 5 = high)
- **Confidence**: 0-100% (your conviction)

#### Optional Fields

- **Leverage**: 1x-20x (for PERP only)
- **Rationales**: Up to 3 bullet points explaining your setup
- **Chart Image**: Upload annotated chart (highly recommended)

### Creating Your First Signal

1. Navigate to **Trading → Trader Dashboard**
2. Click "Create New Signal"
3. Fill out the signal form:

**Example Signal**:

```
Symbol: BTC
Instrument: PERP
Bias: LONG
Entry: 45,000
TP1: 46,000
TP2: 47,000
TP3: 48,000
Stop Loss: 44,000
Leverage: 5x
Timeframe: 4H
Risk: ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5)
Confidence: 75%

Rationales:
1. Strong support at 44k level holding for 3 days
2. Bullish divergence on RSI (4H chart)
3. Breakout above key resistance trendline
```

4. Upload chart image (optional but recommended)
5. Review all details
6. Click "Publish Signal"

### Signal Distribution

Once published, your signal is automatically:

✅ **Sent to Discord** (all followers with notifications enabled)
✅ **Emailed to followers** (if they've enabled email notifications)
✅ **Displayed in follower dashboards** (Trading → Dashboard)
✅ **Added to Signals Feed** (Trading → Signals)

**Delivery Time**: Instant (< 5 seconds)

### Best Practices for Signals

#### Quality Over Quantity

- Don't force trades just to "stay active"
- Wait for high-quality setups
- It's better to post 3 great signals/week than 20 mediocre ones

#### Clear Communication

- Write simple, concise rationales
- Avoid jargon unless necessary
- Explain WHY, not just WHAT

#### Risk Transparency

- Be honest about risk level (1-5 stars)
- Set realistic confidence levels (don't oversell)
- Always include stop-loss

#### Chart Analysis

- Upload annotated charts when possible
- Mark key levels (support, resistance)
- Use arrows to show entry/exit zones

#### Timely Updates

- If setup invalidates, notify followers
- Update targets if market conditions change
- Close signal when all TPs hit or SL triggered
