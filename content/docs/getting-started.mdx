# Getting Started with MyCryptoPilot

Welcome to **MyCryptoPilot** – your gateway to professional crypto trading signals with a risk-first approach.

## What is MyCryptoPilot?

MyCryptoPilot connects retail traders with verified professional traders who share their structured trading signals in real-time. Each signal includes:

- **Entry price** and timing
- **Take-profit targets** (TP1, TP2, TP3)
- **Stop-loss** (SL) level
- **Risk level** (1-5 scale)
- **Confidence** (0-100%)
- **Detailed rationales** explaining the setup
- **Market context** and analysis

## Platform Overview

The navigation is split into dedicated workspaces so you always know where to go:

- **Trading** – dashboards, signal feed, trader marketplace, pricing, checkout, and trader tools.
- **Account** – profile, Discord/email preferences, followed traders, payments, and exchange connections.
- **Crypto School** – structured learning content (coming soon) with progress tracking for Pro/Ultra subscribers.
- **Tax & Declaration** – CSV imports, reconciliations, and future reporting utilities (Ultra roadmap).

## Quick Start (5 minutes)

### 1. Create Your Account

1. Visit [mycryptopilot.app](https://mycryptopilot.app) and click **Sign Up**.
2. Choose email/password, magic link, or OAuth (Discord, GitHub, Google).
3. You start on the **Free plan** with one trader slot and three active signals.

### 2. Complete the Onboarding Essentials

- **Connect Discord** in **Account → Discord** to receive instant alerts and auto-assigned roles.
- **Enable emails** in **Account → Email** if you prefer inbox notifications.
- Review your plan limits under **Trading → Dashboard** and the **Pricing** page.

### 3. Discover Traders & Signals

- Navigate to **Trading → Traders** to browse verified profiles with win rate, payoff, drawdown, and style badges.
- Follow a trader; the Free plan allows one active follow. Pro and Ultra raise the cap (see plan table below).
- Signals you unlock appear in **Trading → Dashboard**, **Trading → Signals**, Discord (if linked), and email (if opted-in).

### 4. Explore the Signals Feed

The feed includes advanced filters for asset, bias, instrument type, trader name, verification, confidence, and publication window. Use them to focus on the setups that match your playbook.

### 5. (Optional) Try Copy Trading & Journaling

- From any signal, open the **Copy Trade** dialog to log the trade manually in your journal today (Pro/Ultra).
- Ultra will unlock automated execution once exchange copy queues ship; the dialog already shows the upgrade requirements.

### 6. Upgrade When Ready

- Visit **Trading → Pricing** to review plans and start the crypto checkout flow.
- Payments support **USDC (Base)** and **USDT (Tron)** with unique deposit addresses, QR codes, and a 15-minute timer.
- Unsure? Use the **$1 Test Payment** plan to validate your wallet before committing.

## Managing Your Account

### Trading Dashboard (followers)

- Tracks active signals, followed traders, plan status, and Discord connection state.
- Offers quick links to marketplace discovery and upgrade CTAs.

### Traders Dashboard (verified traders)

- Provides follower counts, active/total signal metrics, and quick creation of new TradingCard signals.
- Surfaces verification progress, revenue tracking, and performance snapshots powered by your exchange syncs.

### Portfolio Analytics (traders)

- Connect Binance or Bybit read-only APIs via **Account → Exchanges** (plan-gated: Pro=1, Ultra=3).
- Access rich analytics in **Trading → Portfolio**: PnL, Sharpe/Sortino, VaR, drawdown, returns distribution, and trade history.
- Syncs run automatically based on plan refresh intervals (Pro 5 min, Ultra 1 min).

## FAQs

### How often are signals sent?

Depends on the trader’s style:

- **Scalpers**: 5–15 signals/day
- **Day traders**: 2–5 signals/day
- **Swing traders**: 1–3 signals/week

### Can I automate execution today?

Manual journaling is available now. Exchange-backed copy trading is on the roadmap for Ultra subscribers; the UI already reflects upcoming controls so you can plan ahead.

### Which exchanges are supported?

- Signals are compatible with any exchange where the pair exists.
- Verified stats currently sync from **Binance** and **Bybit** via read-only API keys.

### What about the Crypto School and Tax tools?

Both spaces are live in the navigation with **Coming Soon** content. Pro grants early access to courses; Ultra unlocks tax import/report automation once released.

### How do I cancel or pause?

Crypto subscriptions are prepaid. Stop sending renewals and access will expire automatically on the displayed end date.

### I paid the wrong amount—now what?

The checkout engine detects the received USD value:

- **Overpay** → extra access days granted.
- **Underpay** → partial access for the equivalent number of days.
- **Wrong network/token** → reach support with your TX hash for manual review.
