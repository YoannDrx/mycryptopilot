# Getting Started with MyCryptoPilot

Welcome to **MyCryptoPilot** – your gateway to professional crypto trading signals with a risk-first approach.

## What is MyCryptoPilot?

MyCryptoPilot connects retail traders with verified professional traders who share their structured trading signals in real-time. Each signal includes:

- **Entry price** and timing
- **Take-profit targets** (TP1, TP2, TP3)
- **Stop-loss** (SL) level
- **Risk level** (1-5 scale)
- **Confidence** (0-100%)
- **Detailed rationales** explaining the setup
- **Market context** and analysis

## Quick Start (5 minutes)

### 1. Create Your Free Account

1. Visit [mycryptopilot.com](https://mycryptopilot.com)
2. Click "Sign Up" (top right)
3. Choose your sign-up method:
   - Email + password
   - Magic link (passwordless)
   - Discord OAuth (recommended for community access)
   - GitHub OAuth
   - Google OAuth

Your account starts on the **Free plan** automatically.

### 2. Connect Your Discord (Optional but Recommended)

Discord integration allows you to:
- Receive instant signal notifications
- Access the MCP community
- Get auto-assigned Discord roles based on your plan

To connect:
1. Go to **Account → Discord**
2. Click "Connect Discord Account"
3. Authorize the MyCryptoPilot bot

### 3. Browse the Traders Marketplace

1. Navigate to **Trading → Traders**
2. View verified traders with transparent stats:
   - Win rate %
   - Payoff ratio (R:R)
   - Max drawdown

### 4. Follow Your First Trader

1. Click on a trader's profile
2. Review their stats and recent signals
3. Click "Follow" button

**Free plan**: Follow 1 trader, track up to 3 active signals.

### 5. Receive Your First Signal

Signals appear in:
- **Dashboard** (Trading → Dashboard)
- **Signals Feed** (Trading → Signals)
- **Discord** (if connected)
- **Email** (if enabled in Account → Email)

## Using the Risk Console (Pro/Ultra)

The risk console helps you calculate optimal position sizing:

1. Enter your **total capital** (e.g., $5,000)
2. Set your **risk tolerance** (e.g., 2% per trade = $100)
3. Select a signal
4. The console calculates:
   - Position size (in USD or crypto)
   - Quantity to buy
   - Max loss if SL hit
   - Potential profit if TP hit

**Formula**: `Position Size = (Capital × Risk%) / (Entry - SL)`

## FAQs

### How often are signals sent?

Depends on the trader's style:
- **Scalpers**: 5-15 signals/day
- **Day traders**: 2-5 signals/day
- **Swing traders**: 1-3 signals/week

### Can I copy trade automatically?

Not yet. MyCryptoPilot focuses on **education** and **transparency**. Signals are meant to be understood and executed manually, helping you learn.

Auto-trading via API may come in future updates.

### What exchanges do you support?

Signals work on any exchange:
- Binance, Bybit, OKX, Kraken, Coinbase, etc.
- Both SPOT and PERPETUAL markets

Each signal specifies the exact pair (e.g., "BTC/USDT PERP").

### Are results guaranteed?

**No.** Trading involves risk. Past performance doesn't guarantee future results. MyCryptoPilot provides **tools and education**, not financial advice.

Always:
- Trade with risk capital only
- Use stop-losses
- Never risk more than 1-2% per trade

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Crypto subscriptions are pay-as-you-go:
- Simply **stop sending payments**
- Your access expires at the end of your paid period
- No cancellation needed

### I paid the wrong amount, what happens?

The system detects your payment amount automatically:
- **Overpaid**: Get extra days proportionally
- **Underpaid**: Get partial access (pro-rata)
- **Wrong wallet**: Contact support with TX hash
