# Getting started

MyCryptoPilot is a free **Demo / Testnet** product. It does not accept payments,
hold funds, provide financial advice or execute trades.

## Core workflow

1. Create an account and open **Risk Console**.
2. Enter capital, risk percentage, entry and invalidation price.
3. Review potential loss, position size and risk/reward.
4. Optionally inspect a signal and send its values to the console.
5. Optionally connect one Binance or Bybit account with a read-only key.

## Active areas

- **Risk Console** — simulation and saved calculations.
- **Signals** — dated scenarios with source and expiry.
- **Portfolio read-only** — balances and sourced performance values.
- **Traders** — profiles and synchronized history where available.
- **Account** — credentials, preferences, export and deletion.

School, Tax, copy-trading, paid plans and crypto checkout are retired and are
not accessible by navigation or deep-link.

## Safety rule

Never paste a key that can create orders, withdraw or transfer funds. The
connection endpoint inspects permissions and rejects trading scopes, but you
should also verify them in the exchange dashboard before submission.

Past performance and simulated outcomes do not predict future results.
