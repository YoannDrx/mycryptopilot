# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions

### What is MyCryptoPilot?

MyCryptoPilot is a crypto trading signals platform connecting retail traders with verified professional traders. We focus on transparency, risk management, and education rather than promises of guaranteed returns.

### Is MyCryptoPilot free?

Yes, we offer a **Free plan** that includes:
- Follow 1 verified trader
- Track up to 3 active signals simultaneously
- Access to basic features

Paid plans (Pro $49/mo, Ultra $99/mo) unlock more traders, signals, and advanced features like the Risk Console and Trading Journal.

### Do I need crypto experience to use MyCryptoPilot?

**Minimum recommended knowledge**:
- Understand what crypto is
- Know how to buy/sell on an exchange
- Basic concepts: entry, stop-loss, take-profit

**Not required**:
- Advanced technical analysis
- Coding skills
- Prior trading experience

Our Crypto School (Pro/Ultra) helps beginners learn.

### Is this financial advice?

**No.** MyCryptoPilot is an educational platform. Signals are informational and should not be considered financial advice. Always:
- Do your own research (DYOR)
- Trade with risk capital only
- Never invest more than you can afford to lose

### What exchanges does MyCryptoPilot support?

Signals work on **any exchange**:
- Binance, Bybit, OKX, Kraken, Coinbase, KuCoin, Bitget, etc.
- Both SPOT and PERPETUAL (futures) markets

Each signal specifies the exact trading pair (e.g., "BTC/USDT PERP on Binance").

## Using Signals

### How do I follow a trader?

4. Review their stats and recent signals
5. Click "Follow"

### How often are signals sent?

Depends on the trader's style:

- **Scalpers**: 5-15 signals/day (5M-15M timeframes)
- **Day traders**: 2-5 signals/day (1H-4H timeframes)
- **Swing traders**: 1-3 signals/week (1D+ timeframes)

Each trader's profile shows their typical frequency.

### Where do I see signals?

Signals appear in multiple places:

1. **Dashboard**: Trading → Dashboard (main feed)
2. **Signals Page**: Trading → Signals (filterable list)
3. **Discord**: Instant notification in your DM or channels
4. **Email**: Delivered to your inbox (if enabled)

### What does a signal include?

Every signal contains:

- **Symbol**: BTC, ETH, SOL, etc.
- **Type**: SPOT or PERPETUAL
- **Bias**: LONG (buy) or SHORT (sell)
- **Entry Price**: Suggested entry level
- **Take Profit Targets**: TP1, TP2, TP3
- **Stop Loss**: SL to limit downside
- **Risk Level**: ⭐-⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (1-5 stars)
- **Confidence**: 0-100% (trader's conviction)
- **Rationales**: Why the trader took this setup
- **Chart**: Annotated image (optional)

### Should I take every signal?

**No.** Use discretion:

✅ **Take signals when**:
- Risk level matches your tolerance
- You understand the setup
- Market conditions align
- You have available capital

❌ **Skip signals when**:
- Too risky for your comfort (5-star risk)
- You don't understand the rationale
- You're emotionally compromised
- You're overleveraged

Quality > quantity. It's okay to skip signals.

### What if I miss the entry price?

**Options**:

1. **Wait for a pullback**: Price may retest entry level
2. **Skip the trade**: Don't chase, wait for next signal
3. **Adjust your entry**: Enter slightly higher (for LONG) but reduce position size

**Never**: Market buy at a significantly worse price. That's FOMO.

### Can I modify targets or stop-loss?

Yes! Signals are **suggestions**, not commands.

You can:
- Take partial profits earlier (secure gains)
- Move stop-loss to breakeven after TP1
- Scale out gradually at each TP
- Tighten SL based on your risk tolerance

**Remember**: The signal is a starting point. You're in control.

**Active signals** = trades you're currently tracking.

Once Signal #1 hits SL or TP, it closes, freeing a slot for Signal #4.

## Risk Management

### What is the Risk Console?

The **Risk Console** (Pro/Ultra) calculates optimal position sizing based on:

1. Your total capital (e.g., $5,000)
2. Your risk tolerance per trade (e.g., 2% = $100)
3. Signal's entry and stop-loss

**Output**:
- Position size in USD
- Quantity to buy (BTC, ETH, etc.)
- Max loss if SL hit
- Potential profit if TP hit

**Formula**: `Position Size = (Capital × Risk%) / (Entry - SL)`

### How much should I risk per trade?

**Recommended**:
- Beginners: 0.5-1% per trade
- Intermediate: 1-2% per trade
- Advanced: 2-3% per trade (max)

**Never**:
- Risk more than 5% on a single trade
- Go all-in on one signal
- Revenge trade after a loss

### Should I use leverage?

**Leverage amplifies both gains AND losses.**

**Recommendations**:
- Beginners: Avoid leverage (use SPOT)
- Intermediate: Max 2-3x leverage
- Advanced: Max 5-10x leverage

**Never**:
- Use 20x+ leverage (extremely risky)
- Leverage your entire account
- Leverage without a stop-loss

### What if multiple signals hit stop-loss?

This is part of trading. Even the best traders have losing streaks.

**What to do**:
- Review your risk per trade (too high?)
- Check if you're following too many signals
- Take a break if emotionally affected
- Remember: 1-3 losses in a row is normal

**Risk of ruin formula**: With 2% risk per trade, you can survive 50 consecutive losses. With 10% risk, only 10 losses.
